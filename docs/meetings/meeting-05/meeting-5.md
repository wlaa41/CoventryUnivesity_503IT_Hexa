## Academic and Research Considerations

Structured gameplay testing within the team is consistent with formative evaluation methods used in software development projects. By participating in cooperative testing before wider user evaluation, the team can identify and address technical and usability issues early, improving the reliability and validity of findings gathered during formal user testing in subsequent meetings.
