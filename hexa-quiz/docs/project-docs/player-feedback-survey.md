## Key Takeaways

- **Strong overall reception** — 78.6% rated the game *Very good* or *Excellent* (Q7); 84.6% would *definitely* recommend it (Q12).
- **Awareness goal met** — 92.3% said the game improved their cybersecurity awareness at least somewhat (Q11).
- **Standout features** — OTP Email Verification (83.3% Excellent), Game Design & Visual Interface, Voice Narration, and the 3D Bike Reward Game.
- **Most common improvement request** — better/AI **sound and audio feedback**, plus more advanced content and scenario variety.
- **Audience** — mostly aged 18–24 (42.9%); all played in **single-player** mode.
