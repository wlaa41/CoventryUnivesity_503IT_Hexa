# Cybersecurity Questions for Kids (Ages 6–12)

**Topic focus:** Pop-ups, online strangers, passwords, cyberbullying, safe browsing.

---

**1. The Free Toy Ad**

*Tommy is watching a funny cat video on a kids' website. A pop-up appears: "You won a free toy! Click here to claim it!" It asks for his home address.*

**What should Tommy do?**

- A) Click it – free toys are awesome!
- B) Show his mum or dad immediately.
- C) Type his address and then tell his parents.

**Answer: B** – Pop-ups can be tricks. Always ask a grownup first.

---

**2. The Unknown Game Friend**

*Lily is playing an online building game. A player named "CoolCat" sends her a message: "Hi, let's chat. What's your real name and school?"*

**What should Lily do?**

- A) Tell her real name but not the school.
- B) Ignore the message and tell a parent.
- C) Ask CoolCat for his name first.

**Answer: B** – Never share personal info with online strangers.

---

**3. The Password Guess**

*Zac's older brother sees Zac's tablet password "1234". He laughs and says he'll log in as Zac. What should Zac do?*

- A) Nothing – it's just his brother.
- B) Change his password to something harder, like "B3@rCub".
- C) Write the password on a sticker on the tablet.

**Answer: B** – A strong password keeps your account safe, even from family.

---

**4. The "I'm Hurt" Message**

*Maya gets a message from a friend: "I fell and hurt my leg. Can you send me your mum's phone number so I can call her?"*

**What should Maya do?**

- A) Send the number – her friend needs help.
- B) Call her friend on video to check first.
- C) Ask her mum to help before replying.

**Answer: C** – Always let a grownup handle requests for personal information.

---

**5. The Too-Good Deal**

*In a game, a player offers to trade a super-rare pet if Max gives his game password "just for a second".*

**What should Max do?**

- A) Say no – never share passwords.
- B) Share it because the pet is very rare.
- C) Share but change the password back after.

**Answer: A** – Never give your password to anyone, even if they promise something great.

---

**6. The Scary Comment**

*Ella posts a drawing online. Someone comments, "This is ugly. You should stop drawing." Ella feels sad.*

**What should Ella do?**

- A) Delete the comment herself.
- B) Reply angrily.
- C) Show a parent and let them handle it.

**Answer: C** – Cyberbullying is not your fault. Get help from a trusted adult.

---

**7. The Fake Login**

*Kai tries to log into his learning app, but a different screen asks for his username and password again. It looks a bit weird.*

**What should Kai do?**

- A) Type his info – maybe the app updated.
- B) Close the page and tell his teacher.
- C) Ask a friend if they saw the same screen.

**Answer: B** – Fake login pages steal passwords. Always check with an adult.

---

**8. The Stranger Call**

*While playing a voice-chat game, someone says, "Hey, I know your house. Let's meet at the park."*

**What should the child do?**

- A) Leave the game and tell a parent right away.
- B) Ask the stranger where they live.
- C) Say "maybe later" and keep playing.

**Answer: A** – Leave any chat that feels scary and tell an adult immediately.

---

**9. The Shared Tablet**

*Zara shares a tablet with her little brother. He sometimes clicks on ads. What is the best rule?*

- A) Don't let him use the tablet.
- B) Ask a parent to install kid-safe browsing.
- C) Watch him every second.

**Answer: B** – Parental controls help block bad links and ads.

---

**10. The App Download**

*Oliver sees a new game called "Free Robux Generator". His friend says it works. It asks to install something.*

**What should Oliver do?**

- A) Install it – his friend tried it.
- B) Ask a parent before installing any new app.
- C) Install but only once.

**Answer: B** – Free "generators" are usually viruses or scams.

---

**11. The Photo Request**

*A kid in an online art club asks for a photo of Sam's bedroom to "see what his desk looks like".*

**What should Sam do?**

- A) Send a photo of just the desk.
- B) Say no – don't share photos of your home with online friends.
- C) Ask his mum first.

**Answer: B** – Photos of your home can reveal where you live.

---

**12. The "You're a Winner" Email**

*Grandma's email says, "You won a tablet! Click here to get it." The email address looks strange.*

**What should the child do?**

- A) Click it – Grandma would want them to have a tablet.
- B) Tell Grandma about the email before clicking anything.
- C) Forward it to all friends.

**Answer: B** – Scammers pretend to be family. Always check with the real person.

---

**13. The Username Choice**

*Ben signs up for a safe kids' game. He can choose a username. Which is safest?*

- A) BenSoccer7
- B) BenSmithLondon
- C) BennyBoy2024

**Answer: A** – Don't use your real last name or city in usernames.

---

**14. The "Delete Your Account" Lie**

*A message in a game says: "Your account will be deleted in 1 hour. Click here to keep it."*

**What should the child do?**

- A) Click quickly to save the account.
- B) Ignore it – real warnings don't come through game chat.
- C) Ask the game maker for help.

**Answer: B** – Scammers use fear to make you click bad links.

---

**15. The Secret Kept**

*A friend online says, "Don't tell your parents, but I need your pet's name for a surprise."*

**What should the child do?**

- A) Keep the secret – it's a surprise.
- B) Tell a parent immediately.
- C) Give a fake pet name.

**Answer: B** – Secrets that ask for personal information are dangerous. Always tell a grownup.
