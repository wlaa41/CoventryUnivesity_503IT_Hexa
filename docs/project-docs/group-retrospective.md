9.  Key Learnings

Agile is about working in short cycles, getting feedback, and continuously improving

Waterfall delivers once at the end — Agile delivers every 2 weeks with constant feedback

The 3 core Agile techniques — Standup, Sprint Planning, and Retrospective — are essential for any team

Visual slides with icons and pictures are far more effective than text-heavy bullet point slides

Real-life examples from companies like Netflix, Instagram, Uber and Google make learning easier

Clear task assignment and teamwork are critical — every member must own their role

Content depth is important for academic presentations — we will improve this in upcoming parts
