Hexa Quiz Pro: Integrated 3D Bike Reward

This folder contains the supplied Three.js bike game adapted for Hexa Quiz Pro
reward rides.

Integration behavior:
- Hexa launches the bike game after a resolved quiz question.
- Embedded reward rides skip the separate bike-game home and quiz screens.
- Each reward ride lasts 20 seconds.
- Advanced ride physics include acceleration, braking, responsive lane
  switching, animated lean, nitro boost, camera follow, FOV change, and shake.
- Pooled hazards include moving barriers, laser gates, road fractures/oil,
  traffic drones, cyber blocks, and standard barriers.
- Pooled shield, magnet, slow-motion, and double-point power-ups appear during
  rides. Threat warnings, combo scoring, and performance bonuses are enabled.
- The premium cyber city includes three parallax skyline layers, animated neon
  skyscrapers, hologram boards, server towers, firewall walls, data bridges,
  floating data particles, moving lights, and energy tunnel sections.
- Mode visuals are distinct: cyan training city for easy, purple/pink neon city
  for medium, and red/orange firewall zone for hard.
- The road includes animated circuit traces, pulsing grid lines, reflection
  glow, lane-change feedback, and speed-reactive effects.
- Cinematic visual systems include an orbiting countdown camera, boost aura,
  pooled boost sparks, animated shield, camera tilt, and finish celebration.
- The HUD shows speed, timer, nitro, shield, combo, energy, crashes, and score.
- Repeated hazards, rings, power-ups, and particles are pre-warmed and pooled.
- Controls: A/D or Left/Right move lanes, W/Up uses nitro, S/Down brakes,
  and P pauses. The same controls remain available as touch buttons.
- Bike points are returned to the Hexa dashboard and result flow.
- Embedded completion continues to post the message type hexa-bike-complete.
- The exact Three.js runtime is bundled locally as three.min.js.
- The original supplied README is preserved as README-original.txt.
