## Detailed Discussion and Proceedings

### 1.1 Welcome and Programme Context

Sijan Bhandari opened the meeting warmly and confirmed that the project is
registered under the BSc Computing Science programme at Coventry University
Dagenham. The group took some time to walk through the module leader's brief,
which set out expectations around independent research, critical thinking, and
the standard of work required for the final deliverables. Everyone acknowledged
how significant the dissertation component is and agreed to treat it with the
seriousness it deserves.

### 1.2 Team Roles and Governance Structure

Roles were agreed and formally assigned: Sijan Bhandari stepped up as Project
Lead and Chairperson; Johns Jimee took on the dual responsibility of Secretary
and Lead Developer, overseeing documentation and technical reporting; Abdullah
Badhurdeen became our Technical Analyst and QA Engineer, responsible for
testing and defect management; Aditya Bhandari took the Systems Architect role,
handling design, planning, and resource coordination. Sandesh Shah and Nabin
Sunar joined as Project Associates and were fully brought on board from this
meeting onwards, with all documentation shared with them immediately.

### 1.3 Project Vision and Research Questions

Working collaboratively, the team shaped the project's primary research
question: exploring how modern game development frameworks support real-time
interactive systems at undergraduate educational levels. We also drafted
secondary questions covering performance benchmarking, user experience
evaluation, and comparative platform analysis. These questions will anchor
everything we do in the literature review chapter.

### 1.4 Communication and Collaboration Channels

The group agreed on a clear communication setup: Microsoft Teams for formal
scheduled meetings, WhatsApp for quick day-to-day coordination, and a shared
OneDrive folder for version-controlled documents. Meetings will be held
fortnightly, with extra sessions called if needed. Everyone committed to
handling project data responsibly and in line with GDPR obligations.

### 1.5 Ethical Considerations

We reviewed the university's ethics framework together. Since the project
includes user testing, the group agreed to prepare an ethics application form
covering informed consent, anonymisation of participant data, and full
compliance with the BSc Ethics Protocol. Johns Jimee volunteered to draft the
ethics submission before Meeting 3.

## Academic and Research Considerations

The project got underway drawing on established project management principles —
particularly PRINCE2 and Agile Scrum — alongside Saunders et al. (2019)
_Research Methods for Business Students_ to guide our research design. Academic
integrity, proper citation practices, and systematic evidence gathering were
emphasised as non-negotiable from day one.
